Questions people ask before they clock in

One attempt to execute a job. A job that fails twice and succeeds on the third try is three runs. Scheduled fires and queued jobs count the same way.

New jobs wait in the queue and nothing is billed. Jobs resume when the month resets, or when you move to Pro. Your schedules keep their place.

Yes. Set a monthly spend cap on any paid plan. At the cap, Nightshift pauses new runs and sends an alert. You raise the cap or fix the loop. We'll email you at 80 percent, too.

No. Workers can run on Nightshift's machines, or in your own cloud account on Enterprise. Either way, your functions live in your repo and deploy the way they do today.

Most teams move one job at a time. Enqueue from the same code, run the old and new side by side for a night, then switch the old one off. The migration guide has a checklist.

Only as long as your run history setting says. Payloads are encrypted at rest and removed when history expires. You can also mask fields, so card numbers and tokens never reach the logs.
